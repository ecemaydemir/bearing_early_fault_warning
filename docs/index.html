Bearing Early Fault Warning

Source on GitHub

Four bearings share one shaft in the IMS test rig and were run around the clock until one of them broke. This page replays those recordings as if they were arriving live from the machine. A detector learned what “healthy” vibration looks like from the first part of each test, and from then on every bearing gets an anomaly score compared against its own alarm threshold. Pick a test and a detector, press Play (or drag the slider) and watch the status tiles: a bearing turns amber when its score crosses the threshold and red when that persists long enough to raise an alarm. The envelope chart shows which kind of defect the vibration points to, and Reveal ground truth tells you which bearing actually failed and how.

Envelope diagnosis

How far the vibration energy at each bearing defect frequency has moved from its healthy level (z-score, 6-snapshot rolling mean). A curve rising above the dotted line at 3 points to that defect.
